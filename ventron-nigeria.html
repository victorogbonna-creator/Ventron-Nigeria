What is Ventron Nigeria?

Ventron is a Nigerian career-acceleration platform helping young people build practical, industry-relevant skills and move toward meaningful work.

Which program is currently open for enrollment?

Enrollment is open for our instructor-led Frontend Development bootcamp. UI/UX Design and Digital Marketing are coming soon.

How long is the Frontend Development bootcamp?

The bootcamp runs for three months and focuses on practical web development skills, including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

How much does the bootcamp cost?

The program fee is ₦20,000. You can view the payment details on our official Paystack checkout.

How do I enroll or ask about the cohort schedule?

Use the Paystack link to access enrollment, or contact us by email or WhatsApp for current cohort and onboarding details.
